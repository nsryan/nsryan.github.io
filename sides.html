Garlic Loaf

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. 1 3/4 c ~ Hot Water
  2. 1/2 tsp ~ Yeast
  3. 3 c ~ Flour
  4. Salt & Pepper
  5. Minced Garlic
  6. Rosemary
  7. Thyme
  8. Poppy Seeds
  9. Sesame Seeds
  10. Sugar
  11. Oil

Instructions

  1. Bloom the yeast in the water with a little sugar
  2. Mix in the flour, and add a little oil
  3. Knead, then rest in an oiled bowl (covered) for around 14 hours
  4. Punch it down, and let it rest again for another 6 hours
  5. Preheat the oven to 400 F
  6. Knead in the garlic and spices
  7. Bake covered for 35-40 minutes, then bake uncovered until it's done

Garlic Mashed Potatoes

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. 5-6 ~ Potatoes
  2. 3 tbsp ~ White Wine
  3. 2 tbsp ~ Butter
  4. 1/4 c ~ Heavy Cream
  5. 2 tbsp ~ Flour
  6. 3 tbsp ~ Chicken Stock
  7. Minced Garlic
  8. Salt & Pepper
  9. Green Onions
  10. Nutmeg
  11. Oregano

Instructions

  1. Cut the potatoes into more or less even surface area
  2. Put into cold water with salt and bring to a boil
  3. In a separate pot, make a roux with fragrant garlic, flour, and butter
  4. Add in wine and cream, then reduce
  5. Add salt, pepper, chicken stock, and green onions
  6. Heat until the mixture rises then remove from heat and add nutmeg
  7. Strain potatoes when they are fork tender
  8. Mash the potatoes, gradually mashing in the cream mixture until you like the consistency

Braided Pesto Bread

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. 3 c ~ Flour
  2. 2 1/4 tsp ~ Yeast
  3. 2 tsp ~ Sugar
  4. 1 1/4 c ~ Warm Water
  5. 1/4 c ~ Oil
  6. 1 tbsp ~ White Vinegar
  7. Pesto
  8. Salt

Instructions

  1. Bloom the yeast in water and sugar
  2. Separate the water into halves and add vinegar and flour to one half
  3. Add in salt and then alternate oil with the rest of the water
  4. Let rest for 2 hours, covered
  5. Treat it like a cinnamon roll with pesto
  6. Slice the mega roll lengthwise, press it down, and cut the halves again
  7. Braid the resulting strips and brush it in oil
  8. Let it proof again, then bake for 5 minutes at 400 F and finish (2 minutes) at 350 F

Baguette

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. 1 1/2 tbsp ~ Yeast
  2. 2 tbsp ~ Honey
  3. 3 1/2 c ~ Flour
  4. 1 1/2 c ~ Warm Water
  5. Oil
  6. Salt
  7. Cornmeal

Instructions

  1. Make a Poolish the night before
  2. Bloom the yeast and honey with 1/2 c of water
  3. Incorporate flour, then mix in the rest of the water and salt
  4. Knead until smooth, then cover and rest in an oiled bowl for 25 minutes
  5. Divide into two loaves, and fold like an envelope until you have the right shape
  6. Coat the bottom in cornmeal, then place on a baking sheet
  7. Score the top and let it rest (covered)
  8. Preheat to 450 F with a second rimmed sheet under the rack you're going to use
  9. Bake till done (~15 minutes) with ice on the second sheet to steam

Sour Cream & Chive Dinner Rolls

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. 5 1/3 c ~ Bread Flour
  2. 1/2 c ~ Milk
  3. 1 1/2 tsp ~ Yeast
  4. 1 c ~ Sour Cream
  5. 1/4 c ~ Sugar
  6. 2 1/4 tsp ~ Salt
  7. 3 ~ Eggs
  8. 1 stick ~ Butter
  9. 1/2 c ~ Chives (chopped)
  10. Flakey Salt
  11. Pepper

Instructions

  1. Make a tangzhong with milk, 1/3 c flour, 1/2 c water (stirring constantly)
  2. Remove from heat, then (separately) warm 2 tbsp of milk and bloom the yeast
  3. Mix the tangzhong with 2 eggs, 4 tbsp butter, 5 c flour, and the yeast mixture
  4. Take a shaggy dough and knead until tacky (10 min)
  5. Rest in an oiled bowl for 1 - 1 1/2 hours covered
  6. Butter a 9x13, and punch the dough
  7. Form balls, cover and rest in the dish for 45 minutes
  8. Preheat to 375 F, and use the last egg to wash the balls
  9. Salt and pepper, then bake for 25 - 30 minutes

Pumpkin Focaccia Monkey Bread

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. 100 g ~ Oats
  2. 100 g ~ Bread Flour
  3. 300 g ~ AP Flour
  4. 400 g ~ Warm water
  5. Olive Oil
  6. Salt & Pepper
  7. Yeast
  8. Honey
  9. Pumpkin Puree
  10. Minced Garlic
  11. Smoked Paprika
  12. Cayenne
  13. Sage

Instructions

  1. Bloom the yeast in warm water and honey
  2. Put the oats in a blender until a powder forms
  3. Mix all of the flours, salt, and paprika
  4. Mix in the water with some oil
  5. Pull and knead until everything is incorporated
  6. Cover and let sit on the counter for 2 hours
  7. Punch down and let rise in the fridge overnight
  8. Pull out of the fridge and in a different bowl mix oil, pumpkin, and sage
  9. Butter and flour a bundt pan and layer balls of dough with the puree mixture
  10. Cover and let rise on the counter for 4 hours
  11. Bake covered at 425 for 45 minutes, then finish uncovered

Shallot Tarte Tatin

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. Pie crust
  2. Shallots
  3. Minced Garlic
  4. Red Wine Vinegar
  5. Butter
  6. Egg Yolk
  7. Salt and Pepper
  8. Labna
  9. Dill
  10. Oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400F.
  2. Halve the shallots and place, cut side down in an oven-safe pan with butter and sugar.
  3. Sprinkle with salt, pepper, and vinegar.
  4. Cover with pie crust, folding edges underneath the perimeter of the shallots.
  5. Poke holes to vent.
  6. Heat on a stove top until a caramel begins to form and bubble.
  7. Remove from stove top and bake until golden brown according to pie crust recipe.
  8. While the pastry bakes, whip labna with dill, salt, pepper, and oil.
  9. When the crust has 3-5 minutes left, brush with egg yolk.
  10. When crust is golden, remove from oven and allow to cool for 3-5 minutes before flipping on to a plate.

Beet Salad

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. Beets
  2. Goat Cheese
  3. Pistachios
  4. Balsamic Vinegar
  5. Balsamic Glaze
  6. Cherry Tomatoes
  7. Arugula
  8. Olive Oil
  9. Salt and Pepper

Instructions

  1. Roast the beets in the oven at 400F for 20 minutes or until fork tender.
  2. Allow beets to cool, then peel.
  3. Slice cooled beets, and toss in balsamic glaze, oil, salt, and pepper.
  4. Layer the beets at the bottom of the dish you intend to serve the salad in.
  5. Whip or blend goat cheese with oil, pistachios, salt, and pepper.
  6. Spread the cheese on top of the beets.
  7. Slice cherry tomatoes and toss with arugula in oil, balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  8. Place the arugula and tomatoes on the beets and serve.

Focaccia

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. 100 g ~ Bread Flour
  2. 400 g ~ All-purpose Flour
  3. 475 g ~ Warm (110F) Water
  4. 2 tbsp ~ Active Dry Yeast
  5. Sugar
  6. Salt and Pepper
  7. Olive Oil

Instructions

  1. Bloom the yeast in warm water with sugar.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine both flours with salt and pepper.
  3. Pour in the activated yeast and mix until the flour is hydrated.
  4. Continue to mix until the dough becomes smooth and add in a splash of olive oil.
  5. Cover and allow to rest in a warm place for 30 minutes.
  6. Pull sides in and turn the dough.
  7. Cover and rest in the fridge overnight. (For a faster turnaround, rest for 2 hours in a warm place.)
  8. Preheat the oven to 375F.
  9. Turn out the dough into an oiled baking pan and lightly press to dimple and shape.
  10. Cover with oil and season the top with whatever your heart desires.
  11. Bake for 30+ minutes until the bread is golden.

  6. Butter

Instructions

Tomato Soup

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. 24 oz ~ Crushed Tomatoes
  2. 14 oz ~ Chicken Stock
  3. 8 oz ~ Heavy Cream
  4. 1.5 c ~ Rice
  5. 5 ~ Arbol Chiles
  6. 4 tbsp ~ Granulated Sugar
  7. 1 ~ Bay Leaf
  8. 1 ~ Sweet Onion
  9. Bell Peppers
  10. Oil
  11. Minced Garlic
  12. Salt and Pepper
  13. Red Pepper Flakes
  14. Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Dice the bell peppers and slice the onion.
  2. Heat oil in a pot with the arbor chilies, minced garlic, bay leaf, salt, and pepper.
  3. Once garlic begins to brown, add the onion and bell pepper.
  4. When onions and peppers soften, add crushed tomatoes, chicken stock, and rice.
  5. Reduce on medium to low heat until the rice is cooked, then add heavy cream and parmesan.
  6. Serve warm with toast.

Hibiscus Pickles

Back to top

Ingredients

  1. Cucumber
  2. White wine vinegar
  3. Salt & pepper
  4. Mace
  5. Dried hibiscus petals
  6. Granulated sugar

Instructions

  1. Thinly slice cucumbers into even discs.
  2. In a pot, bring the rest of the ingredients to a boil with 2 parts of water and 1 part of vinegar.
  3. Place cucumbers in sealable jars and pour the brining liquid over the top.
  4. Seal and store in the fridge for 1-2 days. Eat within 2 weeks, returning to the fridge between uses.
